Divide from Denominations

Denominations can be a tough idea for some as they show Christianity isn’t completely united and that we do sometimes disagree on different topics. There have been questions like ‘Is Mary the mother of God?’ or ‘Does the Church or Bible have more authority?’ Those are two topics that have divided the church in the past. Some people have realised this and because of this have completely left traditional Christianity with the ‘New Church Movement’. I am a Reformed Anglican coming from a theologically right winged, conservative perspective which will impact my ideas and thoughts on all of these denominations. I will only be writing about the divide that has been caused by denominations, rather than picking on particular denominations to keep the least amount of criticism.
